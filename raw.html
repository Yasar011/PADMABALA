01Brief

Fashion show brief

Create a collection taking the Spectrum 2026 theme (Vibhram) as inspiration.

Maximum six participants: five designers and one stylist. Each team develops a collection of seven garments, judged over three jury rounds.

Concept

This collection deals with what we perceive as reality vs what it actually is. The version of ourselves that we reveal as a package wrapped up with a bow — is it the same on the inside, or is the exterior an illusion created to withhold our true selves?

It explores the themes of duality, delusion and chaos. How we build a wall around ourselves, brick by brick, and what happens when it starts to crack, bit by bit, until there’s no place to hide anymore.

03Ideation & process

Line-up — seven garments, one emotional arc

Sketch to garment

01

The façade

The first garment shows a person presenting themselves in a clean and controlled way. The silhouette looks polished, composed and socially acceptable, representing the outer version we create to hide what truly exists inside.

02

First cracks

The second garment shows the person slowly starting to reveal their true self. Distorted forms and shifting layers represent the first cracks in the illusion, where the hidden identity begins to come forward.

03

Disclosure

The third garment represents the identity being disclosed. Darker layers and heavier construction show discomfort and frustration, as the person feels exposed and wounded, struggling between hiding again or accepting the truth.

04

Outburst

The fourth garment shows anger and emotional outburst. Dramatic movement and heavy construction represent all the suppressed feelings finally coming out after being hidden for so long.

05

Numbness

The fifth garment shows complete numbness. The hood represents refusing to be socially available, while the heavy silhouette shows the weight carried inside. The fringe details represent confusion slowly turning into acceptance.

06

Shock

The sixth garment represents shock after the outburst. The person feels frozen and emotionally drained, the restricted form showing something dying deep inside while silence takes over outside.

07

Acceptance

The final garment represents complete acceptance. The outer layers are removed and the person stands as their true self, no longer hiding behind illusion, fear or social expectations. What was once buried beneath performance and survival is now worn openly, with nothing left to prove and nothing left to conceal.

04Execution & impact

Runway — IGNUS 2026, IIT Jodhpur

Impact

Winning Collection at Spectrum’26 and 1st Runner-Up Collection at IGNUS’26.

Team RAW is a collective of five fashion design students who came together to present the collection: Ayushi Mahajan, Gunisha Rathi, Mahika Rai, Naynika Mitial and Padmabala.
