01Brief

Concept

Contained is a study in emotional discipline as the highest form of authority. In an era of radical exposure, it proposes that the most powerful act is choosing what you reveal.

There is a strength that does not announce itself — the strength of a woman who carries entire worlds within her. The collection is built on a single tension: what is held, and what is allowed to fall free.

Boundaried · Protective · Rebuilt · Quiet · Powerful

02Audience & research

Brand study — Haderlump

“At Haderlump, we see fashion as a timeless force — an extension of the self, a form of armor in an unpredictable world. It protects, empowers, and reveals.”

Haderlump positions fashion as a shield and extension of identity, prioritising emotional depth over trends. Each garment is a vessel of memory and individuality, crafted with integrity. Produced on-demand in its Berlin atelier, the brand embraces conscious creation and rejects the disposability of fast fashion.

Client profile

Age 25–40 · Female (also unisex) · High disposable income · Urban creative hubs. They treat clothing as identity and storytelling, seek experimental silhouettes and concept-driven pieces, and move through fashion weeks, art exhibitions and underground cultural spaces.
