01Brief

Brief

Develop a print and dye-led collection in the language of Aniclothing.

Concept

Fruits and flowers, what if they share the same skin; both reaching, both ripening, both beautifully impermanent. Where colour deepens, softens, and spills into something new. Inspired by the tropics in their quieter hours, saturated in some places, soft and dissolving in others. And then what comes after: dusty, muted, warm like memory, like dusk, like something willingly being let go.

Playful · Ease · Warmth · Abundance

02Audience & research

Brand study — Aniclothing

Aniclothing is a contemporary pret wear brand founded by Shivani Aggarwal, a Parsons School of Design alumna. Known for vibrant self hand-painted prints, minimal silhouettes and maximal personality, the brand bridges Western design sensibilities with cultural roots. Every piece is elegant, effortless and proudly made in India.

Client profile

Young, fashion-forward women who are digitally active and style-conscious.

Age 22–35 · Female · Upper-middle to affluent · Urban India with global appeal. They seek clothing that is visually distinctive, artisanal and expressive, and are willing to invest in premium pret wear that truly stands out.
